About Imbue Healthcare

Who we are

Imbue Healthcare is a South African pharmaceutical and medical device company. We source, register and distribute medicines and diagnostic equipment to the people who need them most, filling the gaps in the South African healthcare system, where patients too often can't reach the treatments that could change, or save, their lives.

Alongside sourcing, registration and distribution, Imbue Healthcare provides specialist commercial support to businesses operating within the pharmaceutical and medical-device sectors. Our capabilities span marketing, sales, patient support and strategy, allowing us to work with healthcare partners across different stages of a product's journey. We combine healthcare industry knowledge with practical commercial support, helping connect products, healthcare professionals and patients more effectively.

WHAT IS A SPIROMETER?

A simple breathing test that measures airflow.

A spirometer measures how much air a person breathes out and in, and how quickly. During a test, the person breathes through a mouthpiece attached to the handset, following a healthcare professional's instructions.

A turbine inside the handset records the airflow. The connected software turns these readings into measured values and graphs, such as Flow-Volume and Volume-Time curves, which the healthcare professional reviews.

FOR HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONALS

HOW SPIROMETRY SUPPORTS COPD DIAGNOSIS

Spirometry plays an important role in confirming airflow obstruction in patients where COPD is suspected. Healthcare professionals consider symptoms such as persistent breathlessness, chronic cough or sputum production alongside relevant risk factors and clinical history.

A key measurement is the FEV1/FVC ratio. In the appropriate clinical context, a post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC below 0.70 confirms persistent airflow obstruction consistent with COPD.

Spirometry results should always be interpreted alongside the patient's wider clinical assessment.

FEV1Forced expiratory volume in 1 second
FVCForced vital capacity
FEV1/FVCKey ratio for airflow obstruction
Post-BDPost-bronchodilator assessment

The Imbue Lung Screening Spirometer supports FVC testing, pre- and post-bronchodilation testing and Flow-Volume and Volume-Time graphs, providing healthcare professionals with practical lung-function data in everyday practice.

THE SOFTWARE

Easy-to-use software that does the work for you.

The spirometer software runs on a Windows PC or laptop and records every test in real time. It displays Flow-Volume and Volume-Time graphs, calculates lung age and compares results against built-in predicted equations.

Results can be auto-interpreted, compared pre- and post-bronchodilation, and tracked as trends over time. Doctors can add notes and print single or all curves.
  1. 1Plug in via USB. The handset draws power from the computer, so no batteries or chargers are needed.
  2. 2Select the test. Choose FVC, SVC or MVV, with auto or manual manoeuvre selection.
  3. 3Review and print. View graphs and auto-interpretation instantly, add notes and print a report.
